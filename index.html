ABOUT OUR SHOP

Welcome to Vanarasi Famous Kabab

Located peacefully in Vanarasi Village (Muduvadi Post, Kolar Taluk), Vanarasi Famous Kabab was established with one passionate mission: to serve the crispiest, juiciest, and most flavor-packed chicken kababs to our local community and highway travelers.

We take deep pride in traditional preparation. Every batch of chicken is sourced fresh every day, cut cleanly, and marinated for hours with our special blend of aromatic village spices, ginger-garlic paste, and crushed herbs. When you order, it is fried fresh to order in hot oil to lock in all the mouth-watering flavors.

Whether you are craving a crunchy evening snack, picking up dinner for your family, or stopping by while passing through Kolar, you will always be greeted with warmth and the irresistible aroma of authentic South Indian kababs.
